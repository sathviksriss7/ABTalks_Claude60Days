# Marketing Strategist : Claude - Prompts

## How to ask Claude — Understanding your audience

Act as a personal branding strategist. My name is Sathvik Sriram, and I'm building a personal brand around Healthcare GenAI Program Management. My story: 6 years turning Healthcare operations chaos into AI-powered systems. Help me define my ideal audience persona (their goals, doubts, and where they spend time online) and explain how my story should shape my content.

## How to ask Claude — Choosing platforms

I'm building a personal brand as Sathvik Sriram around Healthcare GenAI Program Management targeting Product & program leaders in Healthcare Tech, with 3-4 hours/week to work with. I'm considering these platforms: Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, X / Twitter, YouTube, Pinterest, Email Newsletter. For each one, tell me why it is or isn't a good fit for this audience, and which 3 I should prioritize first and why.

## How to ask Claude — Generating content pillars

Generate 6 content pillar ideas for my personal brand as Sathvik Sriram, focused on Healthcare GenAI Program Management, targeting Product & program leaders in Healthcare Tech. For each pillar, explain which goal it supports — authority, relatability, education, or community — and give 2 example post ideas.

## How to ask Claude — Building a 30-day roadmap

Build me a 30-day content roadmap for Sathvik Sriram (personal brand around Healthcare GenAI Program Management), organized by week — not by individual post. Use these content pillars: Audience Education, Thought Leadership, Community Q&A, and these platforms: YouTube, X / Twitter, LinkedIn. Give me a clear goal and strategic focus for each week.

## How to ask Claude — Thinking through a curveball

My personal brand (Sathvik Sriram, Healthcare GenAI Program Management) just experienced this: "A podcast host invites you onto their show." As a marketing strategist, walk me through 3 possible responses and the likely pros and cons of each before I decide what to do.3

## How to ask Claude — Getting a full growth review

Act as a marketing strategist reviewing my personal brand, Sathvik Sriram. Here's my strategy so far: platforms — YouTube, X / Twitter, LinkedIn; content pillars — Audience Education, Thought Leadership, Community Q&A; target audience — Product & program leaders in Healthcare Tech. Give me an honest growth report: my strengths, my biggest blind spot, and 3 lessons I should apply going forward.
