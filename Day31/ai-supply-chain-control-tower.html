    How to run the tower

    • KPIs — Service Level, Customer Satisfaction, Inventory Health and Transportation Efficiency all move with every decision. Operating Cost and Revenue Protected track your financial footprint.
    • Alerts carry a priority (Critical / High / Medium) and a countdown. Left unattended, they resolve themselves — badly.
    • Actions are tailored per alert. The best fix earns the most score and KPI gain; weaker fixes cost more or help less.
    • Ignore drops the alert immediately for a KPI and score penalty. Delay Decision buys a short reprieve but shortens the fuse and mutes future gains on that alert.
    • Difficulty ramps up — more alerts, less breathing room — as the shift goes on.
