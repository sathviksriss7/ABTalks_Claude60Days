Welcome to the Cognitive Pattern Explorer

This is a calm, exploratory journey through a few everyday scenarios and small choices. There are no right answers, no scores to pass or fail, and nothing here diagnoses anything. It simply reflects patterns back to you, gently, in your own language.

Before we begin, choose the atmosphere that feels most comfortable to you right now. This only changes the visual mood — it doesn't affect your results.

Chapter 1 · Discover Your Thinking Style

A few small scenarios. Pick whatever feels most like your first instinct — there's no need to overanalyze this part (unless, of course, that's simply how you think).

Chapter 2 · Choose Your Priorities

Drag the cards so the one closest to how you naturally approach decisions sits at the top, and the least like you sits at the bottom.

Mouse: drag by the handle. Touch: press and drag. Keyboard: focus a card and press ↑/↓ to reorder.

    Chapter 3 · Map Your Thinking

    Imagine you're facing a decision. Drag five of the steps below onto the timeline, in the order you'd naturally move through them. One step won't be needed — that's intentional.

    Keyboard: focus a step card, then press a number key 1–5 to place it in that timeline slot. Focus a placed card and press Backspace to return it to the pool.
