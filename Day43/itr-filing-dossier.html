Workflow summary

The whole case, in one page.

End-to-end path

Collect documents (Form 16, AIS/TIS, broker CAS, VDA statements) → reconcile against AIS/26AS → compute STCG, LTCG and VDA gains separately → pick ITR-2 and populate every schedule → run an independent regime and arithmetic check → file and e-verify within 30 days → track the 143(1) intimation and carry forward any capital losses. The whole loop, done carefully, runs 9–15 hours spread across 2–3 weeks — most of it front-loaded in document collection and reconciliation, not the final submission.
