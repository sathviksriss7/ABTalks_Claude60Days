Tips, Checklist & Resources

Financial literacy for the stage you're in — debt payoff and a stable footing first.

Kill high-APR debt first. Credit card APRs of 36–45% dwarf almost any investment return. Every rupee toward the highest-rate balance is a guaranteed, tax-free "return."
Automate before you can spend it. Move your emergency-fund contribution the day salary lands, not whatever's left at month-end.
Ring-fence family support. Give it a fixed monthly number, agreed with family, rather than open-ended requests — it protects both the relationship and your budget.
3 months before 6. If starting from zero, a 3-month essentials cushion is a realistic first milestone before stretching to 6.
