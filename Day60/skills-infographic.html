Case Study
The Debugging Saga That Proved It All Comes Together
Day 5's AI integration didn't work the first time, or the second. Here's the actual sequence — each step diagnosed from real evidence, not guesswork.
1
Chosen AI provider's free credit unavailable on this account — verified via the actual billing page, not assumed.
2
Pivoted to a genuinely free alternative provider, with explicit sign-off before rewriting the integration.
3
New model silently deprecated for new accounts — a 404 that only made sense after checking the provider's own model list.
4
Upgraded error handling to surface the provider's real error text — turning every future issue into a fast fix.
5
Found the model's reasoning mode silently exhausting the entire response budget before ever answering.
6
Final fix verified live: correct model, right token budget, explicit prompting — shipped and confirmed working.
